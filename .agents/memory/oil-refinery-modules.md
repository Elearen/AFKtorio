---
name: Oil refinery modules
description: The prerequisite and recipe scope for Oil Refinery module upgrades.
---

Oil Refinery module upgrades require the existing `oil-processing` unlock plus the productivity, speed, and efficiency technology for their tier. The installed refinery variant is tracked globally and applies to both Basic Oil Processing and Advanced Oil Processing.

**Why:** Both refinery recipes share the same constructed Oil Refinery count and operating power, while Advanced Oil Processing is also a separate recipe-conversion upgrade.

**How to apply:** Keep refinery module costs and completion keyed to the oil-refinery machine count and variant; do not conflate the module chain with the Basic-to-Advanced Oil Processing conversion.
